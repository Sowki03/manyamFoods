Are your products really preservative-free?

Yes. No artificial preservatives, no refined sugar and no additives. Sun-drying and stone-grinding keep food fresh and nutritious naturally.

How long do they stay fresh?

Powders last 6–12 months in a cool, dry place. Millet snacks are best within 3 months. Every pack has a "best before" date.

How long does delivery take?

4–7 business days across India. Delivery is free on orders above ₹499, otherwise a flat ₹60.

How do I pay?

Pay instantly by UPI (scan the QR at checkout) or place your order and pay later. We confirm every order on WhatsApp.
