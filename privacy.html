Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Manyam Foods ("we", "us") sells traditional food products made by rural women artisans through this website. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and how we protect it. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Information we collect

  • Google sign-in: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We do not receive your Google password or access to your Gmail, contacts or files.
  • Delivery details: your name, mobile number and delivery address, which you enter at checkout.
  • Order details: the products you buy, amounts, coupon used, order status, and the UPI transaction reference (UTR) you enter after paying.
  • Your cart: stored only in your own browser (local storage) until you place an order.

We do not collect card numbers, bank account details or UPI PINs. UPI payments happen entirely inside your own UPI app.

2. How we use it

  • To process, pack and deliver your orders and verify your payments.
  • To show your order history and delivery status in "My Orders".
  • To contact you on WhatsApp or phone about your order.
  • To remember your delivery details for your next order.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Who we share it with

  • Courier partners: your name, phone number and address, only to deliver your order.
  • Service providers: our data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in) and the website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Google provides the sign-in service.
  • Legal requirements: if required by law or a government authority.

4. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed for delivery, returns, accounting and tax purposes (generally up to 8 years for invoices under Indian law). You can ask us to delete your account and personal details at any time; we will delete them except where the law requires us to keep order records.

5. Security

Your data is protected by access rules so that each customer can only see their own orders, and only authorised Manyam Foods staff can see order details. All connections use HTTPS encryption.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw your consent, by contacting us using the details below. You can also remove Manyam Foods' access to your Google account anytime from your Google Account settings.

7. Children

Our website is not intended for children under 18. Orders should be placed by an adult.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.
