Orders & payment

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart on this website, open the cart and choose Checkout. Sign in with Google, enter your delivery details and pay by UPI. You can follow your order anytime in My Orders.

How do I pay?

By UPI only. At checkout, scan the QR code or open Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm with the amount filled in, then enter the 12-digit UPI transaction ID. We confirm your order once the payment is verified.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, from My Orders while it is still "Placed". After it is confirmed, please contact us; once shipped it can't be cancelled.

Can I place a bulk or wholesale order?

Yes. We work with stores, corporate gifting teams and health-focused businesses. Send us a message with the topic "Bulk / wholesale order" and we'll share pricing and minimum quantities.

Do you offer gift hampers?

Yes, curated hampers with a handwritten note for festivals, corporate and personal gifting. Send us a message with the topic "Gift hamper".

Products & ingredients

Are your products natural and preservative-free?

Yes. No artificial preservatives, no refined sugar and no additives. We use traditional methods like sun-drying and stone-grinding to keep food fresh and nutritious naturally.

How long do they stay fresh?

Powders last 6–12 months in a cool, dry place. Millet snacks are best within 3 months. Every pack has a "best before" date.

Are your products safe for children?

Most of our products are child-friendly and made without harmful additives. Millet Crunch is sweetened with jaggery instead of refined sugar. Please consult a paediatrician for infants under 2 years.

How is stone-ground turmeric different?

Industrial grinding creates heat that destroys essential oils and compounds like curcumin. Cold stone-grinding keeps temperatures low, so the colour, aroma and potency stay in the turmeric.

Delivery

How long does delivery take?

4–7 business days across India after your payment is verified. Your tracking number appears in My Orders once the order ships.

Is delivery free?

Free on orders above ₹499. Below that, delivery is a flat ₹60.

Do you ship outside India?

Not yet; we ship within India only. Follow us on Instagram to hear when international shipping launches.

Packaging

Products are packed in food-safe, eco-conscious materials. Powders are sealed airtight and breakable items are bubble-wrapped. If anything arrives damaged, photograph it and tell us within 48 hours of delivery.

Order tracking

Once your order ships, its tracking number appears in My Orders. Use it on the courier partner's website, or contact us if you need help.

Shipping restrictions

  • We ship within India only.
  • Some remote pin codes may take up to 10 business days.
  • Delivery to J&K, the Northeastern states and island territories may take longer.
  • Orders are not dispatched on Sundays and public holidays.

Delayed or lost shipments

If your order hasn't arrived within 7 days of the expected date, contact us. We'll check with the courier and either reship your order or refund it in full.

Returns & refunds

We stand behind every product we make.

✓ Eligible for return or refund

  • Product received damaged or broken
  • Wrong item delivered
  • Product past its best-before date
  • Serious quality issue (unusual smell, texture, etc.)
  • Order not delivered within 12 business days

✗ Not eligible

  • Change of mind after opening
  • Not liking the taste
  • Partly consumed products
  • Damage from improper storage after delivery
  • Claims made more than 7 days after delivery

How to request a return or refund

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery for damage, or within 7 days for other eligible issues.
  • Send photos of the product and packaging on WhatsApp, or describe the issue using the contact form with your order number.
  • We review every request within 1–2 business days and confirm whether it qualifies.

Refund timeline

Approved refunds go back to the UPI account you paid from within 5–7 business days.

Exchanges

If your product qualifies, you can choose an exchange (same or equal-value product) instead of a refund. Exchanges ship within 3 business days of approval, and we cover the delivery cost.
